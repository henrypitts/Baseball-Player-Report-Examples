# Baseball Player Reports & Analytical Research

### Henry Pitts | Baseball Analytics & Player Development

My work focuses on applying statistical analysis, player tracking technology, and data visualization to better understand player performance, identify development opportunities, and support informed baseball decisions.

## Analytical Approach

Across these projects, I emphasize four components of baseball research:

1. **Data Collection & Preparation:** Working with player tracking data, performance metrics, and multiple data sources to construct reliable analytical datasets.
2. **Statistical Analysis:** Applying statistical methods to evaluate player performance, explore relationships, and identify meaningful trends.
3. **Data Visualization:** Presenting complex performance information through accessible charts, graphics, and scouting reports.
4. **Baseball Application:** Translating analytical findings into practical insights relevant to scouting, player development, and roster evaluation.

## Technologies & Data Sources

- **Programming & Analysis:** Python, pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn
- **Player Tracking:** YakkerTech, Blast Motion, pitchLogic
- **Baseball Data:** Baseball Savant, Statcast, TruMedia
- **Reporting:** Statistical visualizations, individual player reports, performance evaluations
